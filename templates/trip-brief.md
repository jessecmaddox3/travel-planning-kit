# Trip brief

> **TL;DR:** Record the group's priorities, constraints and open questions before choosing a place. Use your own private copy of this template.

## What would make this a good trip?

Describe the purpose, preferred pace and the few things people most want to do. Record differences in preferences instead of smoothing them into a false consensus.

## Preference questions

Ask people what would change the plan: travel tolerance, sleeping arrangements, shared meals, activity intensity, quiet time, childcare needs, and deal-breakers. Keep individual answers private where appropriate. Turn the useful conclusions into `plan.preferences`.

## First decisions

List decisions with an owner, urgency, due date, current status and plausible options. Leave unknowns explicit. Do not turn an unanswered question into a confirmed plan.
