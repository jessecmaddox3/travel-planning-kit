# Petition

## Open DeKalb's libraries on Sundays

**To:** DeKalb County Chief Executive Officer Lorraine Cochran-Johnson, the DeKalb County Board of Commissioners, and the DeKalb County Public Library Board of Trustees

Every DeKalb County library is closed on Sunday. For working families, students and anyone who cannot get there on a weekday, that leaves a single weekend window: Saturday, 10 to 5.

We ask you to **fund and open every DeKalb County library on Sundays from noon to 5 p.m., starting in 2027.**

The campaign estimates the cost at $1.65 to $2.1 million a year: between $2 and $3 per resident, and about a third of one percent of the county's General Fund. Gwinnett County already opens every one of its branches on Sunday afternoons.

Our libraries belong to all of us. They should be open on the day many of us are finally free to use them.

**Every library. Every Sunday. Noon to five.**

### Fields to collect
- Name
- ZIP code *(required — commissioners want to see their own residents)*
- Home library branch *(drop-down of all 23)*
- Email *(optional)* with a tick box: "Keep me posted on hearings and votes"
- "How would you use the library on a Sunday?" *(optional, one line — the best source of real stories)*
- "I can help" tick boxes: speak at a hearing · collect signatures · host a Sunday story time · bring my congregation, PTA or group
