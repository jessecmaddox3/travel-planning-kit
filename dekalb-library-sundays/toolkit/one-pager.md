# Open Sundays DeKalb

## Every library. Every Sunday. Noon to five.

**All 23 DeKalb County libraries are closed every Sunday.** For most families, the only weekend hours are Saturday, 10 to 5.

### Who that shuts out
- Parents and kids with a project due Monday
- People who work Saturdays
- Families who keep a Saturday sabbath, for whom the library is closed all weekend
- Students who need a quiet table, a computer or a printer
- The 6% of DeKalb homes with no internet service

### What it would cost
| | |
|---|---|
| Per year, every branch | **About $1.65 million** |
| Per resident | **About $2.15 a year** |
| Share of the county's General Fund | About a quarter of 1% |
| What it buys | 115 more open hours a week, nearly 10% more |

*Campaign estimate. Full workings: the cost model in this repository.*

### Why now
- **Gwinnett County opens every branch on Sunday, noon to 5.** Cobb opens its regional libraries.
- The library's share of the county General Fund has fallen from 5.5% to 4.7% since 2019, and its budget was cut by $910,045 this year.
- DeKalb has paid to restore hours before, in 2016.
- The county adopts its 2027 budget by the end of February.

### What we are asking
**Fund Sunday hours, noon to 5, at every DeKalb County library in the 2027 budget.**
