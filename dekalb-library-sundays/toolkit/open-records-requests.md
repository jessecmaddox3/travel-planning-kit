# Open records requests

Two requests under the Georgia Open Records Act (O.C.G.A. § 50-18-70 and following). Agencies must respond within three business days, either with the records or with a timetable and any cost estimate. Ask for electronic copies to keep costs down.

**Before filing, ask nicely.** Email the library director's office with the same list and a friendly note. Libraries usually share statistics freely, and a formal request reads as adversarial. File only if a week passes without an answer. The campaign needs the library as an ally.

Send request 1 to DeKalb County Public Library's administration and request 2 through DeKalb County's open records portal. Check each website for the current records contact; do not guess an address.

---

## Request 1 — DeKalb County Public Library

**Subject:** Open Records Act request: branch usage, staffing and hours

To the records custodian, DeKalb County Public Library:

Under the Georgia Open Records Act, O.C.G.A. § 50-18-70 et seq., I request electronic copies of the following records.

1. **Door counts** for each library location for July 1, 2024 through the most recent month available, at the finest level kept (by hour, or by day if hourly counts are not kept).
2. **Circulation, computer sessions and Wi-Fi sessions** by location and by day of the week for the same period.
3. **Minimum staffing standards** by location: the number and classification of staff required to open each branch to the public.
4. **Authorised and filled positions** by location and job classification as of the most recent report, including part-time positions and current vacancies.
5. **Any estimate, memo, presentation or analysis** prepared since January 1, 2015 of the cost or feasibility of Sunday hours or of other changes to opening hours.
6. **Records showing when DCPL last offered Sunday hours**, at which locations, and any documents explaining why that service ended.
7. **The library's FY2027 budget request** as approved by the Board of Trustees, including any requested enhancements.
8. **Security staffing and contract costs** by location for FY2026, including the hourly rate paid for contract security guards.

If any record is withheld, please cite the specific exemption. If the estimated cost exceeds $25, please tell me before proceeding. I am happy to narrow the request or to accept existing reports that contain this information in place of raw data.

Thank you for your help.

## Request 2 — DeKalb County Office of Management and Budget

**Subject:** Open Records Act request: library budget detail

To the records custodian, DeKalb County:

Under the Georgia Open Records Act, O.C.G.A. § 50-18-70 et seq., I request electronic copies of the following records.

1. **Object-level budget and actual expenditure** for department 06800 (Library), FY2023 through FY2026: salaries, benefits, overtime, contract services, utilities and supplies by cost center.
2. **The position control report** for department 06800 as of the most recent pay period: each position's classification, grade, full-time or part-time status, and whether filled or vacant.
3. **The pay ranges** for all job classifications used in department 06800, and the county's policy on shift, weekend or Sunday pay differentials.
4. **The FY2027 budget request** submitted by the Library, including enhancement requests, and the Office of Management and Budget's recommendation on each.
5. **Any analysis** prepared since January 1, 2015 of the cost of changing library opening hours.

If any record is withheld, please cite the specific exemption. If the estimated cost exceeds $25, please tell me before proceeding.

Thank you.

### What to do with the answers
- Put the library's staffing minimums, wages and guard rate into the cost model's Assumptions sheet and re-run it.
- If the library has its own Sunday cost estimate, use it in place of ours and say so publicly.
- Door counts by hour show how busy Saturday afternoons are, the best available proxy for Sunday.
