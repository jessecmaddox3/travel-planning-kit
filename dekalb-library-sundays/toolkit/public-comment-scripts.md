# Public comment scripts

For Board of Commissioners budget hearings and Library Board of Trustees meetings. Each runs about two minutes when read aloud. Check the time limit before you go and cut from the middle, never from the ask.

**How public comment works at the Board of Commissioners:** fill in a speaker card and give it to the Clerk before public comment begins. Cards are taken first come, first served, and DeKalb residents speak before non-residents. Each person speaks once per meeting. ([County public participation page](https://dekalbcountyga.gov/node/3348))

**For the Library Board of Trustees:** call library administration ahead of the meeting and ask how to sign up to speak.

**Rules for every speaker**
- Say your name, where you live and your branch in the first sentence.
- Tell one true thing from your own life. Do not borrow anyone else's story.
- Use one number, not five.
- End with the ask, word for word: *"Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library."*
- Bring 10 printed copies of the one-pager for the Clerk.

---

## Parent

Good [morning/evening]. My name is ______. I live in ______ and my library is ______.

[One true sentence about a Sunday in your house. For example: what happens when your child remembers a project at 2 p.m. on Sunday, or what your Saturdays look like.]

Our library is open seven hours all weekend, every one of them on Saturday. On Sunday, when my family finally has time, the doors are locked. That is true at all 23 county libraries.

Opening every branch on Sunday afternoons is estimated to cost between two and three dollars per resident a year. Gwinnett County does it at every branch today.

I am not asking for anything new to be built. The buildings and the books are already ours. I am asking you to unlock the doors.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.

## Student or teenager

My name is ______. I am in ______ grade at ______ and I live in ______.

[One true sentence: when you do your homework, where you go when you need quiet or a printer, what you do when the library is closed.]

Most of my schoolwork is due Monday. Sunday is when I do it, and Sunday is the one day the library is closed.

There are about 164,000 kids in DeKalb County. A lot of us do not have a quiet place at home, and some of us do not have internet at home.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.

## Faith leader or member of a congregation

My name is ______. I [serve / am a member of] ______ in ______.

[One true sentence about your congregation and its families.]

For many families Sunday afternoon, after worship, is the only unhurried time in the week. [If it applies: Members of my community keep the sabbath on Saturday. For them the library is closed for the whole weekend.]

A library that opens at noon on Sunday takes nothing away from worship. It gives families somewhere good to go afterward.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.

## Worker with a Saturday job

My name is ______. I live in ______ and I work at ______.

I work [your schedule]. The library closes at 5 on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, and it is closed on Sunday, my day off.

[One true sentence: what you would use the library for.]

I pay for these libraries like everyone else. I would like to be able to walk into one.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.

## Senior or grandparent

My name is ______. I have lived in ______ for ____ years.

[One true sentence: who you would bring to the library on a Sunday, or what the library means to you.]

Sunday is when my family visits. I would love to take my grandchildren to the library, and I cannot.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.

## The numbers speaker (one per hearing, no more)

My name is ______ and I live in ______. I want to give you the arithmetic.

All 23 DeKalb libraries are closed on Sunday. Opening every one from noon to five is estimated at between one point six five and two point one million dollars a year. That is about a third of one percent of the General Fund.

That estimate includes a Sunday pay differential and a security guard at every branch.

The library's budget has grown since 2019, but more slowly than the General Fund. Its share has gone from five and a half percent to four point seven percent, and this year its budget went down by nine hundred thousand dollars. Sunday hours would cost less than half of what that lost share is worth.

The full model is public. [Say only if true: We have asked the library for its own figures.] If the library's numbers differ, we will use theirs.

Please fund Sunday hours, noon to five, at every DeKalb County library. Thank you.
