# Emails and calls to commissioners

Every DeKalb resident has **two** commissioners: one for their district (1 to 5) and one for their super district (6 or 7). Write to both. Find yours and their contact details on the county's [Board of Commissioners page](https://www.dekalbcountyga.gov/board-commissioners/board-commissioners).

A short personal message from a constituent counts for more than a long form letter. Change the words.

---

## Email from a resident

**Subject:** Please fund Sunday library hours — [your neighbourhood]

Dear Commissioner ______,

I live in ______ and my library is the ______ branch.

[One or two sentences in your own words: why Sunday matters to you.]

Every DeKalb County library is closed on Sunday. I am asking you to fund Sunday hours, noon to 5, at every branch in the 2027 budget. The campaign's estimate is $1.65 to $2.1 million a year, between $2 and $3 per resident, and Gwinnett County already does it at every branch.

Will you support it? I would be glad to hear where you stand.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Street address — shows you are a constituent]
[Phone]

---

## Phone script (30 seconds)

"Hi, my name is ______ and I live in ______, in the commissioner's district. I am calling to ask Commissioner ______ to fund Sunday hours at every DeKalb County library in the 2027 budget. All 23 are closed on Sunday now. Could you tell me whether the commissioner supports that? ... Thank you. My ZIP code is ______."

Write down what the office says and send it to [campaign contact].

---

## Request for a meeting (from the campaign)

**Subject:** Meeting request: Sunday hours at DeKalb libraries

Dear Commissioner ______,

I am a [neighbourhood] resident and one of the organisers of Open Sundays DeKalb, a group of residents asking the county to open every library on Sundays from noon to 5.

We have built a cost estimate from the county's adopted budget and the library's published hours, and we would value 20 minutes to walk you through it and hear your view before the 2027 budget hearings. The one-page summary is attached.

I can meet at your office or by video at any time that suits you. [Two or three specific windows.]

Thank you for your service to the district.

[Name] · [phone] · [email]

---

## What to bring to a meeting
- The one-pager and the first page of the cost model.
- A count of petition signers and survey responses **from that commissioner's district**.
- One constituent from the district.
- One question: *"What would you need to see to sponsor this in the budget?"* Then listen, and write down the answer.

## After every contact
Send a two-line thank-you the same day, restating anything they agreed to.
