# First meeting requests

Three notes to send before the campaign goes public. They are written for one person starting out; change "I" to "we" once that is true. Go to the library first: a campaign that surprises the people who would have to run Sunday service makes them opponents.

---

## To the library director

**Subject:** A resident's question about Sunday hours

Dear Ms. Weissinger,

I live in [neighbourhood] and use the [branch] library with my family. I would like to see DeKalb's libraries open on Sunday afternoons, and I am talking with other residents who feel the same. Before any of us says anything publicly, I want to understand how the library sees it.

I have put together a rough cost estimate from the county's adopted budget and the hours posted on the library's website. I am sure it gets things wrong that you would spot at once. Could I have 30 minutes with you or a member of your team to ask:

- what Sunday service would really take to staff and secure,
- whether the library has looked at this before, and
- what you would want from residents who care about it.

The aim is to argue for money the library would need, not to hand it an unfunded job. I can come to the Administrative Center at any time that suits you.

Thank you for what you and your staff do.

[Name] · [phone] · [email]

---

## To the co-chairs of the Board of Trustees

**Subject:** Sunday hours — request to speak with you before the December 14 meeting

Dear Ms. Armistead and Mr. Paniagua,

I am a DeKalb resident and a regular at the [branch] library. With other residents, I hope to ask the county to fund Sunday hours, noon to 5, at every branch, and I would value your guidance first.

I understand the trustees set policy and present the library's needs to the county. I would like to learn how the board would view a request like this, what it would need to see, and how residents can best support the library's own budget priorities.

Could one of you spare 20 minutes by phone before the December 14 meeting? I would also be grateful to know how members of the public sign up to speak.

With thanks,

[Name] · [phone] · [email]

---

## To Commissioner Robert Patrick (District 1)

**Subject:** Constituent request: 20 minutes on Sunday library hours

Dear Commissioner Patrick,

I live in [neighbourhood] in District 1, and my family uses the [branch] library.

Every DeKalb library is closed on Sundays. I have built a cost estimate for opening all 23 branches from noon to 5: $1.65 to $2.1 million a year, between $2 and $3 per resident. Gwinnett already does this at every branch. A one-page summary is attached.

Before the 2027 budget hearings I would be grateful for 20 minutes to show you the numbers and ask your advice: who else we should talk to, and what you would need to see to support it.

I am available [two or three windows], at your office or by video.

Thank you for your service to District 1.

[Name]
[Street address]
[Phone] · [email]

---

### In each meeting
1. Listen first. Ask what they think before showing anything.
2. Ask what they would need in order to support it, and write it down.
3. Ask who else to talk to.
4. Leave the one-pager. Send thanks the same day.
