# Social posts

Short posts for Nextdoor, Facebook groups, Instagram and neighbourhood email lists. Every figure here is in [Facts and sources](../research/01-facts-and-sources.md). Add a photo you took yourself wherever you can; a locked library door on a Sunday says most of it.

---

**Launch**
All 23 DeKalb County libraries are closed every Sunday. We think they should be open, noon to five, at every branch. It would cost between $2 and $3 per resident a year. Sign and share: [link]

**The comparison**
Gwinnett County: every library open Sunday, noon to 5.
DeKalb County: every library closed Sunday.
We can fix that in the 2027 budget. [link]

**The weekend**
Your DeKalb library is open 7 hours all weekend. All 7 are on Saturday. Sunday, when families finally have time, the doors are locked. [link]

**The price**
What would it cost to open every DeKalb library on Sunday afternoons? About a third of one percent of the county's General Fund. We built the estimate from the county's own budget and published all of it: [link]

**For parents**
The project is due Monday. It is Sunday at 2. The library is closed. If that has been your house, sign this: [link]

**For people who work Saturdays**
If you work Saturdays, your library is open on none of your days off. Tell the county: [link]

**For sabbath-keeping families**
If your family keeps Saturday as a day of rest, DeKalb's libraries are closed for your whole weekend. Sunday hours would change that. [link]

**Nextdoor version (neighbourly, no slogans)**
Hi neighbours. Did you know every DeKalb library is closed on Sundays? A few of us are asking the county to open them from noon to 5, the way Gwinnett does. The county votes on its budget in February. If you would use the [branch] library on a Sunday, would you take one minute to fill in this survey? [link] Happy to answer questions here.

**Before a hearing**
The county holds its budget hearing on [date] at [time], [place]. Two minutes at the microphone from a resident matters more than you would think. We will help you prepare. Reply here or sign up: [link]

**Story time on the steps**
This Sunday at noon we are reading on the steps of the [branch] library, because the inside is closed. Bring a blanket, a kid and a favourite book. [address]

**After a win, or a yes from a commissioner**
Thank you, Commissioner [name], for supporting Sunday hours at every DeKalb library. [link]

---

### Ground rules
- No attacks on library staff or on any official. The library is the thing we are for.
- Only post numbers that are in the facts sheet.
- Photos of children only with a parent's permission.
- Tag commissioners to thank them, never to scold.
