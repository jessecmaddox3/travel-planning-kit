# One-minute resident survey

**Purpose:** measure demand for Sunday hours, by ZIP code and branch. The honest weak point in the case is that nobody has measured Sunday demand in DeKalb. This fixes that.

Keep it to one screen. Offer it by QR code on flyers and with the petition.

---

**1. What is your ZIP code?** *(required)*

**2. Which DeKalb library is closest to you?** *(drop-down of all 23, plus "not sure")*

**3. In the past year, how often did you visit a DeKalb County library?**
- Weekly or more
- A few times a month
- A few times a year
- Not at all

**4. If your library were open Sundays from noon to 5, how often would you go on a Sunday?**
- Most Sundays
- Once or twice a month
- A few times a year
- Probably never

**5. What gets in the way of using the library now?** *(tick all that apply)*
- I work when it is open
- Saturdays are full
- I observe a Saturday sabbath
- Transport
- Nothing, the hours work for me
- Other: ____

**6. Who would come with you on a Sunday?** *(tick all that apply)*
- Children under 12
- Teenagers
- Just me
- An older relative

**7. What would you do there?** *(tick all that apply)*
- Borrow books
- Homework or study
- Use a computer, Wi-Fi or printer
- A children's program
- A quiet place to work
- Other: ____

**8. Anything you want county leaders to know?** *(optional, one line)*

**9. Email, if you want updates** *(optional)*

---

### Reporting the results honestly
- People who answer a library survey are more likely to be library users. Say so when you publish.
- Report question 4 for everyone, and separately for people who answered "not at all" or "a few times a year" to question 3. New users are the strongest evidence.
- Publish counts by commission district, with the number of responses beside each figure.
- Publish the "probably never" share too.
