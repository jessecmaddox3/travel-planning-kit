# Coalition sign-on letter

For organisations: PTAs and school councils, congregations, neighbourhood associations, Friends of the Library groups, senior centres, youth programs, tutoring and literacy groups, chambers and local businesses.

---

[Date]

**To:** The Honorable Lorraine Cochran-Johnson, Chief Executive Officer
The DeKalb County Board of Commissioners
The DeKalb County Public Library Board of Trustees

**Re: Sunday hours at every DeKalb County library**

We are organisations that serve DeKalb County residents. We write to ask that the county fund and open every DeKalb County library on Sundays from noon to 5 p.m., beginning in 2027.

Every one of the county's 23 libraries is closed on Sunday. The people we serve feel it: parents whose only free afternoon is Sunday, students with work due Monday, residents who work on Saturdays, families who keep a Saturday sabbath, and households without internet at home.

The campaign estimates the cost at about $1.65 million a year, about $2.15 per resident. That is a small fraction of the county's General Fund, and it would put buildings and collections the public already owns to work on the day many residents can best use them. Gwinnett County already opens every branch on Sunday afternoons.

We ask that the service be funded at every branch, so that no neighbourhood is left to wait, and that it be staffed in a way that is fair to library employees.

We would welcome the chance to work with you on it.

Respectfully,

[Organisation name] — [signer, title] — [city or neighbourhood]
[Organisation name] — [signer, title] — [city or neighbourhood]

### How to use it
- Send with the one-pager and a two-line personal note saying why this group in particular.
- Ask for a decision by a date, and for one person who will speak at the hearing.
- List signers by commission district when you deliver it, so each commissioner sees who signed from home.
- Organisations that cannot take positions can still share the survey.
