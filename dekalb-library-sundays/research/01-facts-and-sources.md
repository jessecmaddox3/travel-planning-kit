# Facts and sources

Every number the campaign uses, where it came from, and how sure we are. Read on November 21, 2026 unless a different date is shown.

**Confidence labels**
- **Confirmed** — read directly from the official source.
- **Reported** — from press coverage or a search summary of an official page; not yet read directly.
- **Estimate** — the campaign's own calculation. See the [cost model](../cost-model/).

## What Sunday hours would cost

| Outside check: Montgomery County, MD | $1,813,987 | Benchmarks, check 1 |

## Open questions

1. DCPL's own estimate of the cost of Sunday service, and its minimum staffing by branch.
2. Door counts by branch, day and hour, to size demand.
3. Whether DCPL has run Sunday hours before, and why they ended.
4. Current vacancy rate and how long hiring takes.
5. The county's contract rate for security guards.
6. Hearing dates for the FY2027 budget (set after the CEO's recommendation).
7. Which commissioners chair the budget committee and the committee that handles library matters in 2027.
8. A complete list of Sunday hours at Fulton and Cobb branches.

The [open records requests](../toolkit/open-records-requests.md) cover items 1 to 5.
